💡 Useful to know

  • 🔤 The ELOT 743 romanisation is very widely used on Greek road signs, but the UK's PCGN calls it a less reliable guide to pronunciation than the older system it replaced — ΡΟΔΟΣ is Rodos, not "Podos", so reading a sign aloud will not get you understood.
  • 💳 Greek law obliges businesses in the listed activity categories to accept card payment (art. 65, law 4446/2016), so a flat refusal is often illegal — though the duty follows that list, not literally every trader.
  • 💶 A consumer bill of €500 or more must be paid by card, transfer or another electronic means rather than in cash (art. 69 §2, law 4446/2016).
  • 🍞 A cover charge (κουβέρ) is banned, and nothing — bread and bottled water included — may be served and billed without your prior consent (art. 72 of the ΔΙ.Ε.Π.Π.Υ. rules, 2017).
  • 📋 Eateries must display the price list at the entrance and again before you order, and charging above the listed price is a fineable offence — you can check prices from the pavement.
  • 🧾 Issuing the receipt is the business's legal obligation, not yours.
  • 💰 Tipping exists and is recognised in Greek tax law, but there is no dependable customary percentage — ask for the bill (τον λογαριασμό, παρακαλώ) and decide the amount yourself.
  • 🏧 Since 11 August 2025 ATM withdrawal fees are capped at €1.50 for independent operators and drop to zero where a municipal community has only one machine — still carry some cash, because not every small island has a bank branch.
  • 🏥 With an EHIC or GHIC, treatment in public hospitals and health centres is free, but you still pay 25% of medicine costs, €1 per e-prescription (non-refundable) and 15% of tests at a contracted private provider — and at a private provider with no contract the card covers nothing at all.
  • ⛴️ Ferry tickets are personal by law and carry your name and date of birth, so you cannot buy one for someone else or pass yours on.
  • 🌬️ If weather cancels or delays a sailing you keep the right to a refund or rerouting, but EU Regulation 1177/2010 gives no compensation when weather endangers safe operation (art. 20).
  • 💊 A closed pharmacy posts the local duty-pharmacy rota in its window, so the nearest shut door still tells you where the open one is.
