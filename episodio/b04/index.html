The health card and the shape of the hole in it

A European health card puts you inside the Greek state system on the footing of a resident, and that is worth having. It is also less than most people assume. Inpatient care in the ΕΣΥ is free, and so is outpatient care at ΕΣΥ hospitals and at the Κέντρα Υγείας. From there the shares begin: a quarter of the price of medicines, one euro per electronic prescription that never comes back, fifteen percent on tests at a contracted private provider. At a private provider with no contract, the card does nothing. Order the card early and buy travel insurance as well; they are two different products, not two versions of one.

The ticket carries a name

Greek ferry tickets are nominal by law: name, sex, nationality and date of birth of every passenger, recorded at the point of sale. The rule exists because the operator has to know who is on board. What it means for you is small and absolute — you cannot buy a friend's crossing under your own details, and a spare ticket cannot be given away when plans change. Book each passenger as themselves, with the passport spelling, the first time.

Το εισιτήριο είναι στο όνομά μου; — Is the ticket in my name?

How much cash, and why there is a number

A consumer may settle a bill in notes only below five hundred euros. From five hundred up, the payment has to go by card, transfer or another electronic route. Almost nothing on a normal trip touches the ceiling, but knowing it exists explains why a large cash payment sometimes produces a question rather than a shrug — and it is the reason a car hire deposit or a week paid up front is a card job, decided before you pack.

The address, in the alphabet that is on the sign

The Latin version of a Greek street name is what your booking gave you; the Greek version is what is bolted to the corner of the building. Keep both in the same offline note, along with the phone number and the arrival hour. That note is the one thing on this list that works with no signal, no battery on the other side of the conversation, and no shared language.

The call is nine short lines

None of the Greek here is a document list. It closes the fragile gaps in a booking while a phone call still fixes them for free: confirm it, get the exact address, get it in writing, say what time you land, check that someone is awake.
