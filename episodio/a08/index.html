Three parts, one minute

Every goodbye worth making has the same three parts and none of them takes long. Name the person, so it is addressed to somebody rather than announced to a room. Name the thing they did — the room they moved you to, the dish, the bus they told you not to take. And name when you are coming back, with a season attached. Generalised warmth evaporates on the way out of the door; a named dish and a named month stay in the building. This is also why the last morning is worth budgeting fifteen minutes for rather than five.

Ευχαριστώ για όλα. — Thank you for everything.
Θα ξανάρθω το φθινόπωρο. — I'll come back in the autumn.

Why a season, and why that season

Πότε ξανάρχεσαι is asked as a real question, not as a formula, so an answer with a month in it does actual work. There is a second reason to reach for autumn or spring rather than August. Of 29.3 million international air arrivals in 2024, July alone took 16.9 percent and January 2.2 — but the June-to-August share has fallen from 56 percent in 2019 to 50 percent, with spring up 21 percent and winter up 16. The season is stretching at both ends, and the place you have just said goodbye to is quieter and less pressed in those months. Saying θα ξανάρθω το φθινόπωρο is both the warmer answer and the better plan.

The card, the phone number, and the sentence that beats a review

Έχετε κάρτα turns a place you liked into a place you can reach. The card gives you a name to ask for and a number to ring before you arrive, which in a small guesthouse or a family taverna is worth more than any booking platform. Two sentences do the rest: θα ήθελα το ίδιο δωμάτιο του χρόνου, which is a booking and a compliment at once, and θα σας συστήσω σε φίλους, which is the single most valuable thing a visitor can say to a small business and costs nothing to mean.

The four farewells and what each is for

Χάρηκα πολύ που σας γνώρισα closes a first meeting — glad to have met you. Να είστε καλά means be well, it is the warmest ordinary thing in the language, and it is never out of place. Καλή αντάμωση is the goodbye of someone who intends to return: until we meet again, said as a plan rather than a wish. And στο καλό is what you will hear back, a send-off meaning go well. You will also get καλό ταξίδι at the door. None of these needs an answer more complicated than another one of them.

The card in your pocket: eight openings

Everything operational in this course sits on eight frames, and none of them changes shape. Έχετε plus the thing, for whatever the person in front of you might have. Μπορώ να plus the verb, for whatever you want to do. Πού είναι plus the place, in the form written on the sign. Θα ήθελα plus the thing, which is how you order without sounding blunt. Χωρίς plus the ingredient, to subtract instead of describing. Πόσο κάνει plus the thing, before you commit to it. Ψάχνω plus what you are after, when you do not yet know whether it exists nearby. And συγγνώμη in front of any of them, which turns a request to a stranger into a conversation. Eight openings, one card, and the rest is nouns.
