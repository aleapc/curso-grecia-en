Ask, be told nothing, bring something

Τι να φέρω; is the question, and «μη φέρεις τίποτα» is very often the answer. Both halves are part of the same exchange: the question shows you understand there is a form to this, and the refusal is the host's half of it. A box of sweets from a ζαχαροπλαστείο, a bottle, or fruit all work, and Έφερα ένα γλυκό is the whole speech you need at the door.

Τι να φέρω; — What should I bring?
Έφερα ένα γλυκό. — I brought something sweet.

Order in the plural

Ας πάρουμε μερικούς μεζέδες and Θα τα μοιραστούμε όλα are the two sentences that change the shape of a table order. Μεζέδες are small dishes meant to arrive together and sit in the middle; ordering them is a decision about the table rather than about your own plate. It is a way of ordering, not a law of nature — plenty of people at plenty of tables order one main each and that is equally normal.

Ας πάρουμε μερικούς μεζέδες. — Let's get a few mezedes.
Θα τα μοιραστούμε όλα. — We'll share everything.

The toast is a fixed formula

Στην υγειά μας is the full one, Γεια μας the short one you will hear more often, and either is complete on its own — nobody expects a speech attached. Eyes up, glass up, one line, drink. If a second round of toasting comes, the same words work again.

The heritage file, and what it does not say

UNESCO's file for the Mediterranean Diet states that eating together is the foundation of the cultural identity and continuity of communities across the basin, and Greece is among the countries that submitted it. That is real and it is worth knowing. It is a statement about the value of the shared meal — not evidence for any claim about individual plates being unusual, and this course does not make that claim.

How to be full

Δεν μπορώ άλλο, ήταν υπέροχα does two jobs in five words: it declines and it praises, so the refusal is not about the food. Covering the plate with your hand or leaving food to signal you are done both read as puzzles instead. Say it once, warmly, and mean it; you may need to say it twice, and twice is still fine.
