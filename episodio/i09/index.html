The two openings carry the whole day

Ψάχνω puts a thing or a place after it — τη λαϊκή αγορά, κάτι ντόπιο, την πλατεία — and works even when you do not know whether the thing exists nearby. Μπορώ να puts an action after it — δοκιμάσω, καθίσω εδώ, περάσω — and is the question you need at a stall, at a table and at a door. Neither changes shape when you change what follows it.

Ψάχνω κάτι ντόπιο. — I'm looking for something local.
Μπορώ να δοκιμάσω; — Can I try some?

Sunday, and the exception that is older than the rule

Sunday closing dates from 1909, and the first version already exempted restaurants, tavernas, patisseries, cafés and shops selling goods to visitors. The current framework is article 16 of law 4177/2013, with its own list of exceptions — the Sundays before Christmas, the sale periods, the first Sunday of May and of November — and tourist zones designated by ministerial decision. The practical reading is short: the eating-and-drinking half of a town is open on a Sunday and always has been.

What is not settled, and how to handle it

Which other categories of shop may trade on a Sunday has moved more than once, and at least one liberalisation was struck down as unconstitutional. That is a live argument and this course has no opinion in it — but it does mean that any list of Sunday opening rules you read online may be out of date. Είστε ανοιχτά την Κυριακή; asked at the actual counter beats any list.

The coffee is the table, not the drink

Ordering a freddo espresso or a freddo cappuccino buys the seat, and there is no expectation that finishing it means leaving. Nobody will bring the bill until you ask for it. If you want an hour in a square with a book, one glass is the entire cost of the hour, and the second one is optional.

Βόλτα and the square

Πάμε μια βόλτα; is a walk with no destination attached, usually after the heat drops, usually ending at whatever the square is doing. Έχει κάτι απόψε στην πλατεία; is how you find out what that is, and it is a better source than a listings page in a town small enough that the chairs are being carried out in front of you.
