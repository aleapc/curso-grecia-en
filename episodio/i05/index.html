The five, in the order they come

Where are you from, how many days, first time, do you like it, what do you do. They arrive in roughly that order and often two at a time. Load the answers before you need them and the whole exchange takes forty seconds, at which point the conversation can start being about something.

Είμαι από το Λονδίνο. — I'm from London.
Πρώτη φορά, ναι. — First time, yes.

Why they feel personal

Research comparing the two describes Greek as oriented towards positive politeness — involvement and closeness — and English towards negative politeness — distance and non-imposition. The consequence is documented and symmetrical: the English speaker hears intrusion, the Greek speaker hears coldness, and neither reading is right. Married, children, what you earn on a good month: these are openers, not an inspection.

Μου αρέσει turns the sentence round

English puts you in charge of liking: I like Greece. Greek puts the thing in charge: Greece pleases me. That is why the answer to σου αρέσει; comes back as μου αρέσει and not as anything built out of I. Learn the two halves as a pair and you never have to work out which end goes where.

One word to be careful with

Macedonia in Greece is the name of a Greek region, Μακεδονία, whose capital is Thessaloniki. The Prespa Agreement, in force since February 2019, established «Republic of North Macedonia» as the constitutional name of the neighbouring state. So «I'm going to Macedonia» can land two opposite ways. You do not need a view on any of it — you need to say the city, and the ambiguity disappears.

About the English

Greece sits in the high band of commercial English indexes, but those indexes measure people who chose to take an online test, not a population. Speaking is consistently the weakest of the four skills measured. Practically: assume good English at the airport desk and assume none at the village kafeneio, and carry four Greek sentences for the second case.
