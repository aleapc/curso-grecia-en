Θα ήθελα plus the thing

Keep θα ήθελα and put after it anything a table can bring you. Nothing in the opening moves — the two words are fixed, and the item comes in ready-made: έναν καφέ, μια μπύρα, τον κατάλογο, ένα τραπέζι για δύο. Learn each item as a block, the way you learned a phone number, and the frame never asks you to calculate anything at speed.

Θα ήθελα τον κατάλογο, παρακαλώ. — I'd like the menu, please.
Θα ήθελα μια μπύρα. — I'd like a beer.

Why not θέλω

Θέλω means I want, and it is shorter, so it is tempting. It also lands drier in Greek than I want does in English — closer to a statement of fact than to an approach. Θα ήθελα is the conditional, and at a counter it does the job that please does in English before you have even reached the noun. Παρακαλώ then stacks on top and covers please, you're welcome and go ahead, all three.

Ταβέρνα, εστιατόριο, ουζερί, ψησταριά

None of those is a licence class. Greek food legislation puts them all in one bucket — mass catering businesses, under the notification regime of law 4442/2016 — and the difference between the words is trade custom, which is exactly what makes them useful to you. They tell you what to expect on the card and in the room, not what the paperwork says. Τσιπουράδικο is worth knowing as a regional habit above all, strongest around Βόλος and Θεσσαλία.

The list on the wall is a right you can read from the pavement

The trading rules require a price list in Greek, visible at the entrance and before you order, with VAT included — €1,000 where it is missing, €500 for an item charged above what is listed. You do not have to read Greek fluently to use this: find the list, find the line, compare the number. It is the cheapest thirty seconds of the evening.

Bread, water and consent

A cover charge is prohibited. Nothing may be served and charged without prior consent, bread and bottled water expressly included, at €500 per item. This is the opposite of what a traveller arriving from Italy expects, where a declared coperto is lawful. So when the basket appears, the question is not rude — it is the mechanism working. Bread can perfectly well be charged as an item, once it has been offered and accepted.
