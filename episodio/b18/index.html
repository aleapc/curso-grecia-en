The bag first, the key second

Almost every place that rents rooms will keep a bag for the day, and almost none will volunteer it. Ask while the key is still in your hand, because the request reads as part of check-out rather than as a favour asked by someone who no longer has a room. Then ask the second question that matters: μέχρι τι ώρα — until what time — because the luggage room is only useful while somebody is there to open it.

Μπορώ να αφήσω τις βαλίτσες μέχρι το βράδυ; — Can I leave my bags until this evening?
Μπορώ να αφήσω το κλειδί εδώ; — Can I leave the key here?

Πόσο κάνει, one last time

The frame that started at the market closes the trip: πόσο κάνει όλα μαζί at the desk, πόσο κάνει η διαδρομή before the boot closes. Asking the number before you commit costs nothing on the last day either, and the last day is exactly when people stop asking because they are already halfway home in their heads.

Getting out of Athens

From the centre the metro to the airport is €9.00 one way and €16.00 return, and the Express bus is €5.50 — the ordinary €1.20 ticket does not cover the airport line. A normal integrated ticket lasts 90 minutes and, like all of them, it starts counting from its first validation, not from purchase. So buy early if you like, but validate at the moment you actually travel.

A ferry ticket has your name on it

Greek ferry tickets are nominal by law — name, sex, nationality and date of birth are registered with the booking. Two practical consequences: you cannot buy a ticket for a friend using your own details, and an unused ticket cannot be handed to somebody else. If plans change, the conversation is with the company, not with the person you meant to give it to.

The three lines that close a week

Ευχαριστώ για όλα, thank you for everything. Ήταν υπέροχα, it was wonderful. Θα ξανάρθω, I'll come back. The last one is not a contract and nobody hears it as one; it is the standard warm close, and the answer you will usually get is να 'στε καλά or καλό ταξίδι. Καλή συνέχεια — enjoy the rest of it — is the one you can give them back.
